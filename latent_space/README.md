# Latent Space

`01_pgd_trajectory` extracts KeywordCNN's 64-dimensional penultimate-layer embedding, fits a 2D PCA frame on a background cloud of 150 clean clips, and traces how three clips' embeddings move step by step as PGD attacks them. All three change class successfully ("yes" to "on", "no" to "off", "up" to "no"), and two of them end up far outside the background cloud's normal range entirely, not just across a class boundary but off the natural data manifold altogether. The background cloud itself is far more overlapping between classes than the equivalent plots in the vision or text repos (64.0% cumulative variance in two components, versus around 90%+ elsewhere), a direct reflection of this model's more modest 58.2% accuracy: the geometric picture an attack paints depends in part on how well-organized the underlying model's representation already is.
